Understanding Emissions

Why CarbonTrack?

CarbonTrack enables educational institutions to measure, analyze, and report greenhouse gas emissions in accordance with internationally recognized standards. By converting activity data into CO₂e emissions and categorizing them into Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3, the platform helps organizations understand their environmental impact and identify opportunities for emission reduction.

Institutional Data Collection

Centralize electricity, diesel, petrol, LPG, water, paper, waste, refrigerant, and business-travel data from every department in one secure ledger.

Standards-Based Assessment

Convert activity data into precise CO₂e emissions using ISO 14064-1, the GHG Protocol, IPCC guidelines, and government-published emission factors.

Actionable Reporting

Generate scope-wise carbon inventories, executive dashboards, and audit-ready reports aligned to net-zero and sustainability disclosure frameworks.

Our Process

Assessment Methodology

CarbonTrack leverages environmental frameworks established by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) and the EPA to guarantee analytical accuracy.

Step 01

Data Collection

User logs daily variables including energy utility bills, transport fuels, dietary profiles, and recycling habits securely into the engine.

Step 02

Emission Calculation

Our processing layer maps inputs against localized carbon coefficients (e.g., eGRID regional grid emissions, EPA transport fuel density tables).

Step 03

Statistical Analysis

Data aggregates to categorize sources (scopes 1, 2, and 3 emissions) and benchmarks profiles against global climate reduction agreements.

Step 04

Recommendations

AI heuristics supply personalized action plans—like micro-offset investments, solar installation timelines, and low-carb habits.

Data Collection Deep-Dive

Our primary ledger ingests data variables via API hooks. Users can hook smart meters or upload utility statement sheets to automate calculations. All metrics conform to standard equivalent mass parameters of Carbon Dioxide (CO₂e).

Trust & Security

Institution-grade data governance

Purpose-built controls that keep sensitive institutional data secure, auditable, and compliant.

Institution Data Security

All institutional data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Segmented by tenant with zero cross-institution exposure.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions for administrators, sustainability officers, department heads, and auditors — every action is scoped to a role.

Encrypted Records

Utility bills, invoices, and activity logs are stored as encrypted records — no data ever leaves your compliance boundary.

Full Audit Trail

Every data entry, edit, calculation, and report export is logged with timestamp and user attribution for defensible reporting.
