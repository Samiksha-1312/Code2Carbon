Calculation Formulas

Understanding how your emissions are calculated

Emission per Day = Number of Vehicles × Average Distance × Emission Factor

Emission per Month = Emission per Day × 20 (working days)

Emission per Year = Emission per Day × 240 (working days)

Emission per Year (tons CO₂) = Emission per Year (kg CO₂) ÷ 1000
